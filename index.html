The weekly split

Five strength days and two rest days, on a fixed rotation. Strength days run 20 minutes; the walk is 40 minutes on strength days and 60 minutes on rest days.

  1. Monday — Shoulders + triceps
  2. Tuesday — Legs + chest
  3. Wednesday — Rest day · 60-minute walk
  4. Thursday — Shoulders + arms
  5. Friday — Legs + core
  6. Saturday — Arms + shoulders + chest
  7. Sunday — Rest day · 60-minute walk

Weekly muscle-group frequency — Shoulders 3× · Arms 3× · Legs 2× · Chest 2× · Core/obliques 2× · Back 2×

Equipment: every workout in this plan is completable with dumbbells only — plus a resistance band, a chair or step, and a mat, with bodyweight substitutes listed for every exercise. Nothing in the base plan requires anything else. Optional extras: a barbell unlocks the heavier swaps listed on each strength session, and a rowing machine or boxing machine can replace the walk at the same duration — pick them on the day's cards; the coach cues whichever version is selected. Optional wrist and ankle weights have their own guidance — see the panel below. Effort is by feel — RPE / reps in reserve, no prescribed weights. Stop and ease off for sharp pain, dizziness, or chest pain.

Wrist & ankle weights — where they fit

Wrist & ankle weights — where they fit

  • Walks: a light pair on wrists only. In week 1, wear them for the first 15–20 minutes, then take them off and finish the walk; build by ~10 minutes a week until they cover the full 40/60. Keep a natural arm swing and good posture; strap snug but not tight — fingers must move freely, and loosen them if hands swell. Never a heavier pair for walking, and never weights on ankles for walking — ankle weights change gait and pull on ankle and knee joints.
  • Shoulder & arm days (Mon · Thu · Sat): a light pair on wrists for lateral raises and arm finishers, worn for those blocks only (roughly 10–15 minutes of the session). On lateral raises the weights replace the dumbbell or pair with the lightest dumbbell — never stack them on top of a heavy press or curl.
  • Boxing mode (Tue · Fri): a light pair on wrists for the first 4 rounds only, then off. They add load to punches and build shoulder endurance — take them off as soon as punches slow or shoulders start shrugging up.
  • Leg & core work (Tue · Fri): a heavier pair on ankles, for floor-based moves only — glute bridge / hip thrust, dead bug, leg lifts, donkey kicks — worn for those sets only. Not for lunges, step-ups or squats (knee tracking), and not for walking.
  • Rowing mode: don't wear them — they interfere with the handle grip and strap, and strain the wrist.
  • Everyday option: a light pair on wrists or ankles for household activity in 1–2 hour blocks, once the workout use feels easy. Not all day, and never while sleeping.

Progression & safety: start with the light pair everywhere for the first week, even if a heavier set is available. Progress duration first, then weight — one change at a time. One load source at a time: count the weights as part of the load. Take them off for joint pain (wrist, elbow, shoulder, knee, ankle), tingling or numbness. Fit: sit the weight just above the wrist or ankle bone — snug enough not to slide, loose enough that circulation is never restricted.

Reset progress

Progress is stored only on this device, so resets run here in the app. Each button clears ticks stored on this device and asks for a confirming second tap before anything is cleared. Playback speed and the coach setting are always kept.

The same resets can be triggered by adding a reset option to the page address: ?reset=today, ?reset=today-lesson, ?reset=day:2026-10-18 (append :lesson, :strength or :cardio to clear one part of that day), ?reset=week, or ?reset=all. The option is applied once, on load, then removed from the address.

The program Beginner · Intermediate · Advanced

Three phases, four week-templates each. A phase takes at least two weeks and only unlocks the next when every portion — every strength block, cardio phase and lesson — is ticked complete. Anything unfinished rolls forward and simply extends the phase. Tap any day to preview it; locked phases are read-only.

Every day of all twelve weeks — Beginner, Intermediate and Advanced — carries its full episode queue. Progress, done ticks, and playback position are saved on this device, so the strength and walk chunks can be split across the day and the audio picked up where it left off.
